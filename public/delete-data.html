Delete your DungeonRoamer data

DungeonRoamer is a walking role-playing game for iPhone and Android, made by one independent developer. Playing needs no account: the game signs you in anonymously.

The quickest way: open the game, go to Options, then Account and cloud backup, and choose Delete my cloud data and account. Everything the game stored online about you is erased. Your heroes on your own phone stay until you remove the game.

What you can delete, and how

  • Everything online at once: Options, Account and cloud backup, Delete my cloud data and account. This erases your cloud backups, the keys for your friend code and the Realm's Call, your online scores, party data you made, and the anonymous account itself.
  • Only your online scores: Options, Delete my online scores. Removes your entries from the leaderboards and graveyard and turns online scores off.
  • Your friends profile: in the Friends screen choose Leave Friends. Your profile, friendships and blocks are deleted from the server.
  • Your Realm's Call share: in the Realm's Call screen choose Leave the Realm's Call. What the server holds for you is deleted.
  • Anonymous play statistics: switch Share play stats off in Options. This stops anything more being sent and deletes what is waiting on your phone.
  • Data on your phone (heroes, saves, the play log): uninstall the game, or clear the play log in Options.

What is kept

  • Anonymous play statistics already sent carry nothing that links them to you (no name, location, device or account id), so a single person's lines cannot be found afterwards.
  • A report that another player made about you stays until the developer reviews it. It holds only the anonymous ids, the reason and the time.
  • Party lobbies are deleted automatically after 6 hours if nobody starts them, and 48 hours after they start.
  • The services that run the game (Unity and Cloudflare) see your phone's IP address when it connects, as any online service does; the game does not store it.

If you can't use the game

If you have already uninstalled the game, email DungeonRoamer@outlook.com with your hero's name (or friend code) and roughly when you played, and we will remove what we can find. See also the privacy policy.
