DungeonRoamer

Walk the real world to fight monsters, loot dungeons and level up your hero.

A walking roguelike RPG for your phone. Monsters, dungeons and treasure appear around you on a map of the real world; every fight is a turn-based tactical battle on a grid, and when a hero dies, they are gone for good.
