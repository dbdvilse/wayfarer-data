Wayfarer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: Wayfarer uses your location only on your phone and never uploads it. If you take part in the online leaderboards, the game sends an anonymous player ID together with your heroes' names and scores. If you choose to, you can also share anonymous play statistics to help balance the game. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

Who we are

Wayfarer is a walking role-playing game for iPhone and Android, made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at andreas_chevalier@hotmail.com.

Your location

  • Wayfarer uses your phone's location while the game is open, to place you on the map, spawn monsters around you and measure how far you walk.
  • Your location is processed on your phone only. It is never sent to us, never stored on a server and never shared.
  • Your home base (village) and camp positions are saved on your phone, in the game's own save file.
  • To draw the map, the game downloads map tiles for the area around you from a map tile server (currently OpenFreeMap, using OpenStreetMap data). Like any website, that server sees your device's IP address and which map area was requested. We don't receive or keep this information.

Steps and motion

If you allow it, the game reads your phone's step counter to credit walking you did while the game was closed. This is used on your phone only and is never uploaded.

Online leaderboards and graveyard

Wayfarer has world leaderboards and a shared graveyard. For these, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (provided by Unity Technologies) to:

  • create an anonymous player ID for your installation of the game (no name, email, phone number or account is asked for), and
  • upload, with that ID, your heroes' game details: hero name, race, class, game mode, level, experience, score and its parts (such as monsters slain, dungeons cleared and the value of worn gear), kilometres walked, and, for fallen heroes, what killed them.

Other players can see these leaderboard entries. Please don't use your real name or anything personal as a hero name. No location is ever included.

Unity processes this data on our behalf to run the leaderboards. See Unity's privacy policy.

Anonymous play statistics (optional)

The game asks once whether you would like to share anonymous play statistics to help us balance the game. Nothing is shared unless you choose Share, and you can turn it off at any time in Options.

  • What is shared: game events only: which monsters you fought and how the fights went (outcome, damage, spells and ammunition used), levels gained, and shop trades, together with your hero's class, race, level, health and armour. Times are rounded to the hour.
  • What is never shared: your location, your hero's or your name, any device, account or advertising ID, your language or time zone.
  • How it is stored: on our own storage at Cloudflare, with a random per-upload ID that is not linked to you. We do not store your IP address (Cloudflare, like any web host, can see it when your phone connects).
  • Why: only to balance and improve the game. It is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone else.
  • Deleting it: because these statistics carry nothing that identifies you, we cannot find a particular person's records afterwards. Switching sharing off stops anything further being sent.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent any data.
  • We don't collect contacts, photos, health data or payment details.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your heroes, saves and settings live on your phone. Deleting a hero in the game, or uninstalling the game, removes them.
  • Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards run. To have your entries removed, email us with your hero's name (and roughly when you played), and we'll delete them.

Children

Wayfarer is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has uploaded leaderboard entries, contact us and we'll remove them.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently (for example, purchases or multiplayer features), we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes out.
