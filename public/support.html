Wayfarer Support

Wayfarer is a walking role-playing game for iPhone and Android, made by one independent developer.

Need help? Email andreas_chevalier@hotmail.com. It helps to say which phone and system version you have, what you were doing, and the "Game data" version shown in the game's Options.

Common questions

The game can't find my position, or it is wrong.

  • Allow location access for Wayfarer, ideally "Precise location", while the app is open.
  • Try outdoors, away from tall buildings, and wait a moment for the position to settle.
  • The game uses your location only on your phone to place you on the map. See the privacy policy.

The game uses a lot of battery.

  • Open Options and choose "Animations off". Characters stand still, which saves battery.
  • Lowering the effects and music volume to zero does not change battery much, but it is there if you want it.

How do I remove my scores from the online leaderboards?

  • Open Options and choose "Delete my online scores". It removes your entries from the leaderboards and graveyard and turns online scores off. Your heroes and saves on your phone are not touched.
  • If it does not work, email us with your hero's name and roughly when you played, and we will remove the entries.

The game says my game data is out of date, or I can't post my score.

  • Open Options and choose "Check for game data now". A newer version is downloaded and used from the next time you start the game.
  • If a new season has started, update the game data (or the app) before you can post a score for it.

What is "Share play stats"?

  • An optional switch in Options. If you turn it on, anonymous information about fights, levels and shop trades is sent to help balance the game. It never includes your location or name. You can turn it off at any time. Details are in the privacy policy.

I lost a hero.

  • In Hardcore mode a fallen hero is gone for good; that is the point of the mode. Normal mode heroes come back weaker instead. Saves are kept on your phone, so uninstalling the game removes them.

Report a bug or a suggestion

Email the address above. A short description and, if you can, a screenshot are the most useful. Tell us the "Game data" version from Options so we know which edition you were playing.
